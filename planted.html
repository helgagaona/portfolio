The idea

Made for beginners.
Patient by design.

Earthy, handcrafted and warm, Planted explains plant care in plain language so nobody has to feel like they’re failing.

What it does

Three ways Planted helps

Rescue · Collection · Advice

Rescue mode

Tell us what you’re seeing and we’ll help work out what your plant needs, even when the answer is “give it time.”

Your collection

Keep every plant in one cozy place, with its own notes, history and care rhythm.

Friendly advice

Plain-language tips for light, water and soil. No jargon, no judgment.
